Comments are notes or discussion messages attached to tasks.

A comment has:
- Id
- TaskId
- UserId
- Content
- CreatedAtUtc
- UpdatedAtUtc

Comments belong to tasks through TaskId.
A comment can optionally have a UserId to show who wrote it.

Important comment routes:
- GET /api/tasks/{taskId}/comments gets comments for a task.
- POST /api/tasks/{taskId}/comments creates a comment on a task.
- PUT /api/tasks/{taskId}/comments/{commentId} updates a comment.
- DELETE /api/tasks/{taskId}/comments/{commentId} deletes a comment.

Comments are useful for discussing details, progress, or questions about a task.
